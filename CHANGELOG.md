# Changelog

## Unreleased

First public version.

- `/pb` plans the upgrades with the most Pawn gain that fit your budget,
  from your latest Auctionator scan.
- Each upgrade shows its net cost: the AH price minus what a vendor pays
  for it.
- Upgrades that sell to a vendor for nearly what they cost get their own
  list. `/pb near 10s` sets how near (default 5s), `/pb near off` hides it.
- `/pb net`, or the Net cost box, plans by net cost instead of AH price.
- `/pb scan` prices each version of random-suffix items with the AH open.
